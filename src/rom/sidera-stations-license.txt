COMNAP's stated terms: "Data may be freely used for educational and

These terms apply to the COMNAP-derived station data, including the station
records in sidera.cty. They are separate from GPL-3.0-only for SIDERA's original
code. The bundled station dataset is not licensed under the GPL and is not
unrestricted open data. Preserve this notice with redistributed station data.

Selection: 22 open research stations south of 60 degrees S, including seasonal
stations and Antarctic islands. Stations without confirmed coordinates,
sea-level elevation or civil time zone are omitted. The reviewed selection and
exclusions, source links, map identifiers and snapshot hash are recorded in
src/data/stations.json. This is a filtered snapshot, not a complete directory
of all operating facilities. No automatic online updates are performed.

Changes: short ASCII names, ISO operator codes, Antarctica [AQ] grouping,
coordinates rounded to 0.1 arcsecond, representative sea-level heights in
integer metres, and assignment to existing IANA time-zone identifiers.
Arctowski is the AQ default. Coordinates are representative facility points,
not navigation fixes. Heights from COMNAP marked WGS84 or without a datum are
only used when an independent source corroborates a sea-level height; some
values are replaced by operator-reported heights.

Time-zone assignments use IANA tzdb Antarctica comments and station-specific
research/operator sources linked in stations.json. They are not inferred from
longitude or operator nationality. IANA data is public domain; the packaged
tzdata notices are in sidera-timezones-license.txt. Fixed Etc/GMT zones describe
documented operating time, not a reconstruction of station history. Regional
zones retain their IANA history, which need not describe an unoccupied station.
Troll retains IANA's UTC/UTC+2 approximation. Future time follows bundled rules.

Independent facts used to confirm or replace elevations and time zones:
- Arctowski: DOI 10.1007/s00484-025-03099-9 (2026), 2 m a.s.l., UTC-3.
- Dome Fuji: DOI 10.1029/2009JD011886, UTC+3.
- Neumayer III: DOI 10.5194/tc-15-4745-2021, UTC.
- Princess Elisabeth: International Polar Foundation, BELARE news January
  2026, same civil time zone as Belgium.
- Amundsen-Scott: USAP Participant Guide (2022), 2835 m a.s.l.
- Dumont d'Urville: French Polar Institute station page, 20 m altitude.
- Rothera: British Antarctic Survey meteorological database, 16 m altitude.
- Scott Base: Antarctica New Zealand station page, 10 m above sea level.
- Troll: Antarctic Treaty inspection ATCM36 attachment 109, 1275 m a.s.l.
Only factual catalogue values are incorporated; no photographs, diagrams or
article text from these sources are included.
