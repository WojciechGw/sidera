SIDERA world locator

Land contours: Natural Earth, 1:110 million land, version 4.1.0.
Downloaded 2026-11-13 from:
https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/110m/physical/ne_110m_land.zip
Natural Earth vector and raster data are in the public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The normalized rings and source archive SHA-256 are in src/data/world-land.json.
SIDERA rasterizes these contours to 300 x 150 pixels. Geographic grouping is
original SIDERA code, separate from Natural Earth; it does not use national
borders. Europe/Asia uses a simplified Urals, Caspian, Caucasus and Turkish
Straits boundary. Oceania includes New Guinea and Pacific islands, including
Hawaii. Greenland is grouped with North America; Madeira and the Canaries with
Africa. These are schematic, conventional groupings, not territorial claims.
