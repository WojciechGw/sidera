SIDERA city catalogue - attribution and provenance

This notice covers the populated-place records. Antarctic research stations
in the same sidera.cty file have separate terms: sidera-stations-license.txt.
The 21 geographic reference points in the internal XX group are documented in
src/data/locations.json. Nemo coordinates follow NOAA Ocean Service:
https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/nemo.html (48 deg 52.6 min S, 123 deg 23.6 min W).
South Pole's representative 2835 m height follows the USAP Participant Guide
(2022). The poles and Null Island use their geographic definitions. UTC and
zero ocean heights are explicit application conventions. These point records
are separate from the COMNAP station selection.

Additional summit facts: Peakbagger.com, individual peak URLs in locations.json.
Stonehenge and Greenwich elevations: UNESCO/IAU Portal to the Heritage of
Astronomy, https://web.astronomicalheritage.net/ (entities 49 and 34).
Historic Greenwich meridian coordinates: https://www.thegreenwichmeridian.org/
Machu Picchu height: https://www.machupicchu.gob.pe/history/?lang=en
Nordkapp plateau height: https://www.nordkapp.no/places/north-cape-plateau/
Machu Picchu, Nordkapp and Cape Horn coordinate facts: OpenStreetMap contributors
via Mapcarta; https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright (ODbL 1.0).
Cape of Good Hope and Longyearbyen: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).
Cape Horn: selected land point about 26 m east of the small southern lighthouse;
55 m Copernicus GLO-90 terrain via Open-Meteo, retrieved 2026-11-15.
Great Pyramid reference point: selected ground north of the pyramid footprint;
58 m Copernicus GLO-90 terrain via Open-Meteo, retrieved 2026-11-15.
No building height is added. Coordinates and heights are rounded, approximate
point references, not survey-grade measurements. Source URLs and modifications
are recorded per location in src/data/locations.json. No source images or
descriptive articles are included.

345 selected populated places, covering 193 United Nations members and the two
non-member observer states (Holy See and State of Palestine).
Catalogue snapshot: 2026-11-09. It is not a historical list of capitals.

Changes: selected and transliterated names, curated regional additions,
capital/default/seat classification, coordinates rounded to 0.1 arcsecond,
fixed-record binary conversion. Source IDs and heights are in cities.json.

Elevations use the GeoNames point elevation when available, otherwise its
SRTM3/GTOPO30 terrain model. Heights are approximate point elevations, not
uniform elevations of entire cities. Display precision is not accuracy.
Four missing coastal DEM heights (Punta Arenas, Suva, Monaco, Tromso) use
Copernicus GLO-90 via Open-Meteo, queried at the same points on 2026-11-09:
https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/elevation-api
Open-Meteo data: CC BY 4.0. Copernicus DEM attribution:
European Union, Copernicus Land Monitoring Service.

Polish additions: 18 regional capitals (Warsaw already present), plus
Sosnowiec (GeoNames 3085128) and Radziechowy village (3087431), both in
Slaskie, admin1=83. New Polish heights use DEM terrain at the reference
point, including Szczecin (25 m), not the city-wide elevation field.

The Hague, Putrajaya, La Paz, Cotonou, Abidjan and Ramallah are additional
government seats, not labelled as national capitals. Bern and Yaren are
country-code defaults labelled Seat. East Jerusalem is the Palestinian
declared capital as recorded by GeoNames; Ramallah is also selectable.
Names and inclusion of locations do not determine territorial status.

The data are supplied as-is. Rebuild offline with tools/cities.py; the
reviewed source snapshot is data/cities.json.
