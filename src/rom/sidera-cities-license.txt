SIDERA city catalogue - attribution and provenance

345 selected populated places, covering 193 United Nations members and the two
non-member observer states (Holy See and State of Palestine).
Catalogue snapshot: 2026-11-09. It is not a historical list of capitals.

City coordinates (WGS84), names, country information and most elevations:
GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/countryInfo.txt
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/PL.zip

Changes: selected and transliterated names, curated regional additions,
capital/default/seat classification, coordinates rounded to 0.1 arcsecond,
fixed-record binary conversion. Source IDs and heights are in cities.json.

Elevations use the GeoNames point elevation when available, otherwise its
SRTM3/GTOPO30 terrain model. Heights are approximate point elevations, not
uniform elevations of entire cities. Display precision is not accuracy.
Four missing coastal DEM heights (Punta Arenas, Suva, Monaco, Tromso) use
Copernicus GLO-90 via Open-Meteo, queried at the same points on 2026-11-09:
https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/elevation-api
Open-Meteo data: CC BY 4.0. Copernicus DEM attribution:
European Union, Copernicus Land Monitoring Service.

Polish additions: 18 regional capitals (Warsaw already present), plus
Sosnowiec (GeoNames 3085128) and Radziechowy village (3087431), both in
Slaskie, admin1=83. New Polish heights use DEM terrain at the reference
point, including Szczecin (25 m), not the city-wide elevation field.

Scope:
https://research.un.org/en/unmembers/observers
Geographical coverage guides the additional cities. Timezone identifiers
select civil-time rules from the separately bundled IANA database; see
sidera-timezones-license.txt. Astronomical calculations continue using UTC.

The Hague, Putrajaya, La Paz, Cotonou, Abidjan and Ramallah are additional
government seats, not labelled as national capitals. Bern and Yaren are
country-code defaults labelled Seat. East Jerusalem is the Palestinian
declared capital as recorded by GeoNames; Ramallah is also selectable.
Names and inclusion of locations do not determine territorial status.

The data are supplied as-is. Rebuild offline with tools/cities.py; the
reviewed source snapshot is data/cities.json.
